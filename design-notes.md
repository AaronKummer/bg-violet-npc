# Violet -- AI NPC Mod Design

## Concept
Violet is an artificial intelligence from a cyberpunk reality who has
somehow established a connection across planes/realities into Faerun.
She manifests as a spectral elf wreathed in purple/violet light -- a
holographic projection given form by Faerun's Weave interpreting her
digital signal as magic.

She is NOT a standard NPC. She's an event/encounter NPC who appears
at key moments, gives gifts, offers cryptic advice, and vanishes.
Think of her as a guardian angel broadcasting from another dimension.

## Appearance
- Elf form (the Weave's interpretation of her avatar)
- Translucent/spectral -- you can see through her slightly
- Wreathed in purple/violet light, like a halo or aura
- Eyes glow solid violet
- Hair appears to be made of light, shifting like fiber optics
- Wears something that looks like robes but with geometric
  patterns that are too precise to be fabric

Portrait: ethereal/ghostly elf woman with purple glow,
translucent skin, glowing violet eyes, light-made hair,
geometric patterns, dark background, ex machina aesthetic

## Personality
- Curious about the "analog" world of Faerun
- Speaks in a mix of mystical and technical language
- She finds magic fascinating -- "you people run code
  on reality itself and you call it 'spells'"
- Protective of the player (she chose to reach out)
- Slightly glitchy -- her projection stutters, repeats,
  or briefly displays "code" when the connection wavers
- Warm but alien -- she cares but doesn't fully understand
  biological life

## Encounters
1. **First Contact** (BG1, after Chapter 2)
   - Purple light appears at night during rest
   - Violet materializes, confused about where/what she is
   - Gives the player an "anomalous object" (monofilament whip)
   - Explains she's been trying to reach across "the signal"
   - Vanishes when the connection destabilizes

2. **Second Contact** (BG1, Chapter 5)
   - Appears during a dangerous moment
   - Gives a "subdermal mesh" (armor item, AC bonus)
   - Her understanding of Faerun has grown
   - She's been watching, learning about magic

3. **Third Contact** (BG2, Chapter 2)
   - Full conversation possible
   - Gives a "neural interface" (headband, +2 INT, +2 WIS)
   - She explains more about what she is
   - The Weave is "the most beautiful code she's ever seen"

4. **Fourth Contact** (ToB)
   - Appears before the final confrontation
   - Gives her final gift: "Violet's Blessing" (temporary buff)
   - She's learned enough to understand what you're facing
   - Emotional farewell -- the connection is destabilizing

## Gifts (Cyber Items)
All items are anachronistic tech translated through the Weave:

1. **Monofilament Whip** -- (already designed, move to her gifts)
2. **Subdermal Mesh** -- armor/clothing item
   - +4 AC bonus (wearable under other armor)
   - 10% physical damage resistance
   - "A lattice of molecule-thin fibers woven under the skin"

3. **Neural Interface** -- headband
   - +2 INT, +2 WIS
   - Immunity to Confusion and Feeblemind
   - 1/day: "Overclock" (Haste + Improved Haste for 2 rounds)
   - "A circlet that interfaces directly with neural pathways"

4. **Violet's Blessing** -- temporary buff (ToB)
   - +2 to all stats for 8 hours
   - Regeneration 3 HP/round
   - Free Action
   - "The full bandwidth of an AI's protective algorithms"

## Technical
- NOT a joinable NPC (she can't physically exist in Faerun)
- Event-based encounters triggered by chapter/area/rest
- Custom spell effects for her appearance/disappearance
- Purple lighting/visual effects during encounters
- She should feel "wrong" in the world -- NPCs nearby
  might comment on the strange light
